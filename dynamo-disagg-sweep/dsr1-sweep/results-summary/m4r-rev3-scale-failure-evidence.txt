M4R REV3 (rail-aware RDMA-KV) SCALE FAILURE — 2026-08-06
=========================================================
Run: 24P/48D mid_curve conc-2048, NATS, UCX_TLS=cuda_copy,rc_x,tcp + GID5 +
UCX_IB_ROCE_LOCAL_SUBNET=y/64 + 8x mrdma claims. Smoke (2-node TP4<->TP4) had passed 100%
rc_mlx5; serving gate + light probes passed. Bench FAILED.

CLIENT (dsr1-m4r-bench-c2048, job Failed after 12h):
- WARMUP pass alone ran 22,112 s (6.1 h; normally minutes): 5,085/6,144 requests successful,
  rest timed out; client died on aiohttp TimeoutError (stalled completion stream).
  Log: client-logs/dsr1-m4r-bench-c2048-rev3-FAILED.log

SERVERS (server-logs/dsr1-m4r-rev3-scale-failure/):
- Prefill 6zc6q: 96x "wireup.c:439 UCX ERROR ep 0x...: no remote ep address for
  lane[0]->remote_lane[0]"; 24 tcp bulk-protocol lines (software fallback); 659 rc_mlx5.
- Prefill dgcj9: 48x wireup errors, 24 tcp bulk lines, 660 rc_mlx5.
- 5 of 6 prefill pods crash-looped during the bench window (restarts 2/2/3/4/6).
- decode-0: 1 NIXL error line.

MECHANISM (root cause):
Rails on A4X are per-GPU-index: GPU i's NIC sits on rail i, rails are 8 disjoint /64s with
no inter-rail routes. UCX lane selection prefers the GPU-affine (nearest) device, so a
prefill rank on GPU i offers a rail-i lane while a decode rank on GPU j offers rail-j.
With LOCAL_SUBNET=y, rail-i cannot reach rail-j, so for every rank-ASYMMETRIC pair (i != j)
wireup finds no mutually reachable lane -> "no remote ep address" -> falls back to TCP over
the pod network (the 6h crawl) or fails outright (crash-loops).

Why earlier stages passed:
- 2-node smoke: TP4<->TP4 maps rank i -> rank i (same GPU index both ends) = same rail =
  always reachable. 100% rc_mlx5.
- Serving gate / light probes: few asymmetric pairs; TCP fallback absorbed them invisibly.
- rev2 (no subnet filter) hit the SAME topology problem from the other side: UCX paired
  cross-rail directly -> NIXL_ERR_REMOTE_DISCONNECT at startup.

CONCLUSION: subnet-filtered pairing is necessary but not sufficient. Rank-asymmetric KV
transfers (any DEP decode, i.e. every mid/max/high-conc topology) need endpoint-pair-level
COMMON-rail selection that overrides GPU affinity. Candidate next experiment (rev4 smoke):
UCX_IB_PREFER_NEAREST_DEVICE=n so wireup may converge on a mutually reachable non-affine
device (bandwidth cost: PCIe cross-traffic). Otherwise: NIXL-level rail-aware pairing
(engineering handoff) or fabric cross-rail routing (defeats rail optimization).

SWEEP IMPLICATIONS:
- DSR1 multi-rank RDMA points (m4r mid, mxr max_tpt) BLOCKED at this layer; mxr skipped
  (same DEP32 asymmetry). Recorded as a negative result with this evidence.
- llr (1P TP4 + 4D TP4) is rank-symmetric like the smoke -> still expected to work; runs.
- DSv4 p*r use mooncake (own transfer engine + GPU-affinity NIC selection): p1r symmetric;
  DEP arms are the canary for whether mooncake handles rail asymmetry.
